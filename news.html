News

2026

Awarded a Gordon and Betty Moore Foundation Postdoctoral Fellowship (2026–2027) to reconstruct ancient ocean chemistry and date Antarctic ice cores.

2026

Presented two talks at Goldschmidt 2026 in Montreal, on the 550-million-year seawater lithium record and on high-resolution impurity mapping of million-year-old Antarctic ice, and convened the session “Seawater Chemistry Through Time.”

2026

“Decoupling of Neogene seawater lithium isotopes from uplift-driven weathering” published in Nature Communications.

2025

Invited talk at the Gordon Research Conference in Maine on secular variation in Phanerozoic seawater lithium and its links to Earth’s climate states.

2025

Began coordinating the Environmental Geology and Geochemistry Seminar (EGGS) lecture series in the Department of Geosciences at Princeton.

2024

Began an NSF Office of Polar Programs Postdoctoral Research Fellowship at Princeton, developing a strontium-isotope chronometer for stratigraphically disturbed Antarctic blue ice.

2024

“The evolution of Earth’s surficial Mg cycle over the past 2 billion years” published in Science Advances.
