Teaching & Mentoring

Princeton University — Mentoring

  • Mentored undergraduate and graduate researchers in isotope geochemistry and LA-ICP-MS.
  • Developed labs on fluid inclusions, halite geochemistry, and MATLAB modeling.
  • Trained graduate students Ishraque Fairuz (cryo-cell LA-ICP-MS analysis of Antarctic ice) and Gemma Sahwell (lithium isotope column chemistry and ICP-QQQ-MS).

Teaching Experience

  • Binghamton University, Graduate Teaching Assistant (2016–2021): Geochemistry (GEOL 470/570); Planet Earth (GEOL 111); Earth's Interior (GEOL 214); Climate and Paleoclimate (GEOL 414/514); Geology of the Solar System (GEOL 102). Taught lab sections of 15–30 students each semester for five years.
  • Eritrea Institute of Technology, Lecturer & TA (2011–2015): General Geology (GEOL 201) — developed all course materials from scratch; Geological Map Interpretation (GEOL 232) — redesigned the lab around student-built 3D block models; Introduction to Rock-Forming Minerals (GEOL 212); Introduction to Geophysics (GEOL 461); led eight undergraduate field camps for 100+ students.

Mentoring & Research Supervision

Graduate students & postdocs

  • Princeton: Ishraque Fairuz and Gemma Sahwell (current).
  • Binghamton: graduate student Eugene Doyle; postdoctoral researchers Will Arnuk and Emmanuel Guillerm; laboratory manager David Collins — all in LA-ICP-MS analysis of fluid inclusions in halite and gypsum.

Undergraduate mentoring

At the Eritrea Institute of Technology, supervised more than 40 undergraduates in field mapping, thin-section preparation, and senior thesis research. Alumni now work at the Geological Survey of Eritrea and in the mining industry, or are pursuing graduate degrees in Europe, North America, and Australia.
