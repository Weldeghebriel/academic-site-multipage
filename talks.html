Talks & Service

Selected Conference Presentations

  • Goldschmidt 2026 (Montreal): High-resolution 2D impurity mapping of million-year-old Antarctic ice by cryo-LA-ICP-MS; and A 550-million-year record of the concentration and isotopic composition of lithium in seawater.
  • Gordon Research Conference 2025 (Maine): Secular variations in Phanerozoic seawater lithium and links to Earth's climate states and the carbon-silicate cycle (invited).
  • Goldschmidt 2025 (Prague): A 3-billion-year lithium isotope record from marine dolostones (session convener).
  • Goldschmidt 2024 (Chicago): Secular variation of lithium concentration and isotopic composition of Phanerozoic and Neoproterozoic seawater (invited).
  • EGU General Assembly 2024 (Vienna): Variability in Sr and Li composition of ancient seawater from halite fluid inclusions (session convener).
  • GSA Annual Meeting 2023 (Pittsburgh); Goldschmidt 2023 (Lyon); Chapman Conference on Hydrothermal Systems 2023 (Agros, Cyprus).
  • AGU Fall Meeting 2021 (New Orleans); AGU Fall Meeting 2019 (San Francisco); GSA Annual Meeting 2019 (Phoenix); GSA Northeastern Section 2018 (Burlington, VT).
  • 3rd World Young Earth Scientists Congress 2014 (Dar es Salaam, Tanzania).
