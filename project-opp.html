NSF OPP Fellowship — Antarctic Blue-Ice Geochronology

This project develops an ⁸⁷Sr/⁸⁶Sr chronometer from sea-salt inclusions to date >1 Ma blue-ice stratigraphy using LA-ICP-MS imaging. Antarctic blue-ice areas expose ice 1–6 Myr old, but this ice is folded and stratigraphically disturbed, so conventional layer-counting loses resolution beyond ~1 Ma.

  • Funding: NSF OPP Postdoctoral Fellowship (Award 2420291, $359,487, 2024–2026)
  • Carried out through the NSF Center for Oldest Ice Exploration (COLDEX)
  • Methods: LA-ICP-MS, Sr isotopes, micro-analytical mapping
  • Outcomes: 2D isotope maps, age-depth models, calibration datasets
  • Payoff: a direct atmospheric sample from the mid-Pliocene, the most recent interval with CO₂ near present-day levels
