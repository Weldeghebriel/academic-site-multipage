Mass Balance Modeling of Seawater Evolution

Reading a seawater-chemistry record is only half the problem; the other half is explaining it. I use forward and inverse mass-balance models to quantify the roles of continental weathering, seafloor hydrothermal systems, volcanism, and subduction in regulating seawater composition and atmospheric CO₂.

  • Approach: reservoir–flux models, Monte Carlo ensembles, data assimilation
  • Outputs: time-dependent [Li⁺], δ⁷Li, ⁸⁷Sr/⁸⁶Sr, δ²⁶Mg
  • Shows secular δ⁷Li variation requires only modest (~2×) changes in spreading/weathering — not a fundamental carbon-cycle reorganization in the early Paleozoic
