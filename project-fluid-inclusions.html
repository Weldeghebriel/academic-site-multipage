Fluid Inclusions as Ancient Seawater Archives

Fluid inclusions in marine halite are tens-of-micrometer droplets of ancient evaporated seawater — the most direct record of ocean chemistry through the Phanerozoic and Neoproterozoic. We measure major, minor, trace elements, and isotopes directly in these inclusions.

  • Techniques: LA-ICP-MS; cryo-SEM-EDS; microthermometry
  • Targets: [Li⁺], δ⁷Li, [Ca²⁺], [SO₄²⁻], Sr and trace metals
  • Scale: 1,371+ inclusions from 32 marine evaporite basins spanning 550 Myr
  • Applications: secular seawater trends; hydrothermal vs. weathering controls
